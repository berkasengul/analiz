# Ephemeral Dyadic: satış mesajları

**Neden bu firma:**
- Sanatçı Sinan Saul'un İstanbul'un sanayi bölgesindeki küçük bir sanat atölyesinde kurduğu parfüm projesi.
- Manifestosu var: "You will not remember the chemical on your skin but you will remember how it made you feel."
- 10 koku, hepsi 50 ml Eau de Parfum, 170 €. Ayrıca 10 x 2 ml keşif seti (80 €), dünyaya ücretsiz kargo.
- Her kokunun ürün sayfasında kendi şiiri ya da alıntısı ve nota piramidi var. Her kutunun da kendine özgü siyah-beyaz bir mürekkep deseni var.
- Satış noktaları: Türkiye'de Shopigo ve Wunder; İtalya (Antonioli dahil), Almanya, ABD, İspanya, Körfez, Avustralya, Baltık ve daha fazlası.
- Sitesi Wix'te, sade bir mağaza. Kutu desenleri ve şiirler sitede küçük görsel ve düz metin olarak kalıyor.

**Kime:**
- **Mail:** info@ephemeraldyadic.com (sitenin alt kısmında). Kurucu Sinan Saul'a hitaben.
- **Instagram:** sitede bağlantı yok, adres bilinmiyor. Göndermeden önce doğrula.
- Marka uluslararası satıyor ama kurucu İstanbul'da: Türkçe yaz, altına İngilizcesini ekle.

>
> In short:
>
> - **Every scent in front of its own artwork:** The black-and-white ink artwork from each box becomes a large mural behind its scent. The whole scene is black and white; the only colour is the perfume in the bottle, standing on a grey stone plinth under a spotlight. Moving from Dark Dreams to Psychic Vibrations, the artwork and the light change.
> - **The bottle in your hand:** You can turn the bottle with a finger; its back label carries the notes. "Spray it" sends a mist towards the screen and reveals the scent's profile.
> - **Each scent's own words:** The poems and quotes from your site (Ozymandias, Another World, Psychic Vibrations…) sit next to each scent, with its notes pyramid.
> - **Ephemeral · Dyadic · How it made you feel:** Your manifesto unfolds in three steps as you scroll, each with a scent on stage.
> - **Scent finder:** Three questions ("Which moment?", "What pulls you?", "What should stay on skin?"), matched with your notes; the visitor's scent lands on the plinth.
> - **Manifesto and stockists:** The studio's story, your stockists from Türkiye and Italy to Germany and the US, and an FAQ (free shipping, discovery set).

> The site is in English and Turkish and works the same way on phones. The bag links to each product page on ephemeraldyadic.com, so it sells without changing your current store.
